## Start with the source licence

The displayed Starter Pro price covers the source package and the rights in its
[licence](/license). Your order and supplied terms define update access, support
period and any service commitments. This guide does not replace those terms or
reduce rights granted with an existing purchase.

Installation guidance and reproducible defects in the supplied starter belong
to the support scope of your purchased offer. Purchase access and recovery use
[purchase support](/orders/support). Share your version, minimal steps,
diagnostic code and a redacted screenshot; keep secrets, session cookies and
customer data out of support messages.

## Discuss a scoped implementation

For an assisted starting point, [contact PyColors](/contact) with the workflow
you need and the environment you plan to use. A written engagement can define:

- **Licence and baseline:** the source release and licensed entity involved.
- **Initial configuration:** the agreed database, authentication, email and
  billing setup for your target environment.
- **Targeted adaptation:** a named workflow, integration or design change with
  explicit acceptance criteria.
- **Validation and handover:** agreed checks, operating notes and ownership of
  credentials, backups and alerts.
- **Bounded follow-up:** the support period, included assistance and escalation
  route agreed for that engagement.

The implementation price is quoted after scope review. It is separate from the
source-only checkout. Business-specific features, hosting, ongoing operation and
upgrades of customized code require an agreed scope. No default SLA, response
deadline or 24/7 coverage is promised by this page.

## Prepare for handover

Confirm who operates the application, manages provider accounts and can restore
the database. Record the delivered revision, acceptance results and outstanding
operator setup. The [validation guide](/docs/starter-pro/release-readiness) and
[operations guide](/docs/starter-pro/operations) make that review concrete.
