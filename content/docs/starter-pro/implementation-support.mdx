Installation guidance and reproducible defects in the supplied starter belong
to the support scope of your purchased offer. Purchase access and recovery use
[purchase support](/orders/support). Share your version, minimal steps,
diagnostic code and a redacted screenshot; keep secrets, session cookies and
customer data out of support messages.

## Define the engagement

A written engagement can define the following steps with PyColors:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Licence and baseline

    Identify the source release and the licensed entity involved.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Initial configuration

    Agree on the database, authentication, email and billing setup for your
    target environment.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Targeted adaptation

    Name the workflow, integration or design change and its acceptance criteria.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Validation and handover

    Agree on checks, operating notes and ownership of credentials, backups
    and alerts.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Bounded follow-up

    Record the support period, included assistance and escalation route for
    that engagement.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for handover

Confirm who operates the application, manages provider accounts and can restore
the database. Record the delivered revision, acceptance results and outstanding
operator setup.
