These are starting service fees. The source licence and third-party provider
costs are itemized separately in your quote. Confirm the deliverables, target
environment, acceptance checks, total price and included follow-up in writing
before work begins. A quote can exceed the displayed starting price.

## Request a written proposal

Use the [project brief form](/request-a-quote) to describe the workflow, choose
setup or business adaptation, and share an indicative budget. Company and
desired timeline are optional. Keep credentials and customer data out of the brief.

The confirmation provides a request reference after the email provider accepts
the transmission to PyColors. It does not certify inbox delivery or confirm a
price, booking or delivery date. We review the request before agreeing on a
written scope. Use the displayed reference when following up with PyColors.

## Standard starter support

Installation guidance and reproducible defects in the supplied starter belong
to the support scope of your purchased offer. Purchase access and recovery use
[purchase support](/orders/support). Share your version, minimal steps,
diagnostic code and a redacted screenshot; keep secrets, session cookies and
customer data out of support messages.

## Define the engagement

A written engagement can define the following steps with PyColors:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Licence and baseline

    Identify the source release and the licensed entity involved.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Initial configuration

    Agree on the database, authentication, email and billing setup for your
    target environment.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Targeted adaptation

    Name the workflow, integration or design change and its acceptance criteria.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Validation and handover

    Agree on checks, operating notes and ownership of credentials, backups
    and alerts.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Bounded follow-up

    Record the support period, included assistance and escalation route for
    that engagement.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adapt one shared foundation

Use the included workspaces, projects, assigned tasks, due dates and discussions
as the first reusable business example. Keep branding and product settings in
their documented entry points, and build domain-specific workflows on that
baseline. A specialized workflow or integration belongs in the agreed scope and
acceptance criteria. Review [included capabilities](/docs/starter-pro/what-is-included)
before defining your adaptation.

## Inspect an example delivery

The [implementation example](/starters/pro/implementation-example) turns the two
engagements into concrete briefs: one configured preview environment, or one
internal client-delivery workflow. It shows the scope, environment record,
acceptance evidence and team handover contents.

[Download the editable example handover](/starters/pro/implementation-example/handover)
to review the format. It is an illustrative document: its result fields are marked
**NOT RUN**, with spaces for the actual revision, environment, tester, outcome
and evidence. Complete these at acceptance for the agreed scope. Keep credentials
in your credential manager and record only their ownership and location.

## Prepare for handover

Confirm who operates the application, manages provider accounts and can restore
the database. Record the delivered revision, acceptance results and outstanding
operator setup.
