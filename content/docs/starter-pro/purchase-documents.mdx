This guide concerns buying Starter Pro from PyColors. The
[billing system included in Starter Pro](/docs/starter-pro/billing) is for
customers of the app you build; it does not provide a billing portal for your
PyColors purchase.

## Know which document you need

- **PyColors access email:** names your product, includes an order reference,
  and links to your download access. It does not include a payment receipt or
  invoice PDF.
- **Payment receipt:** a separate record of a successful payment. If you receive
  one, keep it with your purchase records. Contact purchase support if it is
  missing or its details need review.
- **Invoice:** if you need an invoice or particular company billing details,
  ask purchase support to confirm what is available for your purchase.

The order reference in the access email helps support locate your purchase;
it is not an invoice number. Receiving an access email does not guarantee that
a separate receipt or invoice email will arrive.

## Before purchasing for a company

If your purchase depends on a particular invoice format or billing detail,
contact [purchase support](/orders/support) before paying. Describe the document
you need and let support confirm what can be provided. This guide does not
promise a particular invoice format, automatic invoice delivery, or a deadline
for issuing or correcting a document.

## After checkout

1. Check the inbox and spam folder for the email address used at checkout.
2. Keep any payment document you receive separately from your access email.
   Keep the access link private: it grants access to the product.
3. If a receipt or invoice is missing, or a document has incorrect details,
   open [purchase support](/orders/support). Include the checkout email, product,
   approximate purchase date and order reference if available, and explain
   which document or correction you need.

You can ask for help without an order reference. Do not send card details,
passwords, API keys, or claim/download links. Redact these from screenshots too.
Support can review the request; a correction or delivery time is not guaranteed
by this guide.

## Missing access or uncertain payment?

[Purchase recovery](/orders/recover) resends eligible product access. It does
not issue or resend receipts and invoices. Use the
[purchase recovery guide](/docs/starter-pro/purchase-recovery) when the ZIP or
access email is missing.

A missing document does not establish that payment failed. If payment status is
unclear, contact support before buying again.
