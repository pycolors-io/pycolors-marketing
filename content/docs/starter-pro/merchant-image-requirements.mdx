## Merchant image contract

Starter Pro’s Merchant image guidance is intentionally conservative:

- repository-owned Merchant assets should be at least `500 × 500 px`
- `1500 × 1500 px` is the preferred canonical target when practical
- 1:1 assets are preferred for product feeds

<Callout title="Validation boundary">
  Google Merchant Center can also ingest externally hosted images. When those
  dimensions cannot be verified from repository evidence, Starter Pro
  documents the boundary instead of guessing. Only repository-owned assets are
  validated automatically in-repo.
</Callout>

## What this protects

This contract is meant to keep future Merchant uploads from drifting below the
minimum required dimensions before Google’s January 2027 enforcement date.

## What remains external

If a Merchant integration references hosted images outside the repository, the
repository can document the requirement but cannot prove the remote asset
dimensions without external evidence.
