This guide explains how you receive the **Starter Pro ZIP bought from PyColors**.
You do not need a PyColors account to claim or recover your purchase.
It does not configure subscriptions or digital-product delivery for an app you
build with Starter Pro. For that app's subscription foundation, see the
[billing guide](/docs/starter-pro/billing).

<Callout title="Already paid but cannot access the ZIP?">
  Check the inbox and spam folder for the email used at checkout, then use
  [purchase recovery](/orders/recover). If you are unsure whether payment
  completed, contact [purchase support](/orders/support) before paying again.
  Recovery does not complete an unpaid checkout.
</Callout>

## From payment to your ZIP

1. **Complete checkout and check confirmation.**

   Pay through Stripe Checkout using an email address you can access. PyColors
   processes the payment confirmation on the server before creating purchase
   access. A browser redirect alone does not prove payment or delivery succeeded.
   Payment confirmation, access-email delivery, and a completed download are
   separate steps.

2. **Find the claim email.**

   PyColors sends an access email to the address used at checkout after purchase
   access is created. Email delivery can fail even when your purchase access
   exists. Check spam if it is missing; neither the confirmation page nor email
   submission proves that a message reached your inbox. No exact email-arrival
   or download-completion time is guaranteed by this guide.

   The claim email is for product access. Receipts and invoices are separate;
   see [purchase receipts and invoices](/docs/starter-pro/purchase-documents).

3. **Open the original access link.**

   Use the link in the claim email rather than opening the claim route manually.
   The link contains the private access token needed to verify your purchase
   access. On **Your access is ready.**, check that the product is **Starter Pro**
   and keep the order reference for support.

   Access links currently expire 24 hours after they are issued. If yours has
   expired, request a fresh link through [purchase recovery](/orders/recover).
   Keep claim and download links private; do not put them in public issues,
   screenshots, or shared documentation.

4. **Download, save, and start setup.**

   Select **Download package** on the claim page. PyColors validates the link
   and active purchase access on the server before serving the private ZIP.
   An access-ready page does not prove that the file finished downloading.
   Check that the download completed, save the archive safely, and unzip it.

   Continue with [Getting started](/docs/starter-pro/getting-started), including
   its first-session checkpoints. Downloading the source code does not provision
   or deploy your SaaS. Configure and validate your own environment and
   integrations before following the
   [production checklist](/docs/starter-pro/production-checklist).

## When a delivery step fails

### Payment is pending or uncertain

Do not buy again just to obtain another access email. Contact
[purchase support](/orders/support) with your checkout email, the product name,
and the approximate purchase date. The
[interrupted checkout page](/checkout/cancel) does not verify payment status or
cancel an existing payment. Follow the
[purchase recovery guide](/docs/starter-pro/purchase-recovery) for the distinction
between retrying an unpaid checkout and recovering an existing purchase.

### No claim email arrives

Check the checkout email and spam folder, then open
[purchase recovery](/orders/recover) and select **Resend access link** with that
same email. Eligible active purchase access can receive a new claim email.

A recovery confirmation does not prove that a purchase exists or that an email
reached your inbox. Repeated requests may be temporarily limited. Follow the
[purchase recovery guide](/docs/starter-pro/purchase-recovery) for retry steps;
contact support if access is still missing rather than making another purchase.

### The page says "Invalid access link"

This can happen when the claim link is missing its token. Open the original
link from your claim email. Do not construct a token or copy an example URL.
If you cannot find a usable link, request a fresh one through purchase recovery.

### The page says "Access link unavailable"

The claim page could not verify the link. It may be expired or invalid, or the
service may be temporarily unavailable; this message does not identify the
cause or prove that payment failed. Try again shortly or request a fresh access
link. If the new link also fails, contact purchase support.

### The download fails after access is ready

Check your connection and retry the download. If the link has expired, request
a fresh one. If the file remains unavailable or the ZIP cannot be opened,
contact support with the product name and the error you see. Access verification
and successful file delivery are separate checks; do not pay again to retry.

### The link is for another product or the checkout email is inaccessible

Contact purchase support. Recovery currently selects the most recent active
purchase access associated with an email; it cannot select a different order or
change the checkout email. It also cannot restore inactive purchase access.
The [purchase recovery guide](/docs/starter-pro/purchase-recovery) explains these
limits and the support handoff.

## Ask for purchase support safely

Open [purchase support](/orders/support) for the email contact and fallback
instructions if your email app does not open. Include the checkout email,
**Starter Pro**, approximate purchase date, order reference if available, the
failed step, and what you already tried.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, API keys, or claim/download links.
Remove those details from screenshots too. Support can review the purchase or
access issue; no response-time or resolution-time guarantee is promised.
Requesting a new access link does not resend receipts or invoices.
