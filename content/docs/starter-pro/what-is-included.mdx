## What is included in Starter Pro

Starter Pro includes the production foundations that usually slow SaaS launches down.

It is not a finished SaaS business.
It is not a generic UI bundle.
It is not a collection of disconnected demos.

It is a **production-ready SaaS foundation** built around the layers most paid products need before launch:

- authentication
- billing
- protected routes
- backend-ready structure
- secure delivery
- app foundations

<Callout title="Core idea">
  Starter Pro includes the infrastructure layer that makes a product launchable.
  You still build the product logic that makes it valuable.
</Callout>

## Connected product workflows

- **Accounts:** guided onboarding, editable profile/avatar, language/time zone,
  revocable sessions and ownership-aware account lifecycle.
- **Teams:** multiple workspaces, live role checks, invitations, notifications
  and paginated activity with actors and resources.
- **Project work:** descriptions, status, responsible members, archiving, tasks,
  calendar due dates and paginated discussions. Archived projects reject changes
  until restored.
- **Appearance:** palettes, typography, geometry, interface scale and sidebar
  controls with browser persistence and matching loading states.
- **Business recipes:** [client tracking, support requests and internal validation](/docs/starter-pro/recipes),
  with guided adaptations of the project/task/comment model and explicit domain boundaries.
- **Operations:** diagnostics, health/readiness, controlled error references,
  alert hooks and a synthetic PostgreSQL restore drill.

Billing belongs to the **user account**. Workspace/resource quotas are enforced
on the server when configured and are **uncapped by default**. The source licence
and your application's subscription are separate. Purchase recovery and ZIP
delivery are operated by PyColors; the PyColors storefront is not an application
module supplied in your starter ZIP.

Review [validation evidence](/docs/starter-pro/release-readiness),
[customization and upgrades](/docs/starter-pro/customization-and-upgrades) and
[implementation scope](/docs/starter-pro/implementation-support).

## What this means in practice

Starter Pro is commercially useful because it removes the systems work that paid products usually need before launch:

- login
- sessions
- billing
- protection
- subscription rules
- backend wiring
- secure delivery

That work rarely differentiates the business, but it must be done well.

Starter Pro gives you a serious baseline so you can focus your time on:

- product value
- customers
- positioning
- workflows
- differentiation
- distribution

### What you are buying

You are not buying a finished SaaS.

You are buying a faster path through the layers that are usually:

- repetitive
- security-sensitive
- revenue-critical
- annoying to rebuild
- expensive to patch later

<Callout title="The better question">
  The question is not “How much stuff is included?”

The better question is:
“Does this remove the work that would delay launch?”

</Callout>
