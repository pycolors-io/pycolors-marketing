Use [purchase recovery](/orders/recover) if your Starter Pro access email is
missing, you lost the original link, or your download link has expired.
You do not need a PyColors account or another purchase to request access.

This guide concerns the Starter Pro ZIP you bought from PyColors. It does not
reset a password or manage subscriptions inside an app built with Starter Pro.

## If checkout did not complete

If the checkout button cannot open the payment page, try again later. If you
already attempted a payment or are unsure whether it completed, open
[purchase support](/orders/support) before paying again.

For a declined payment or interrupted authentication, follow the instructions
on the payment page. You can retry when you know no payment completed and none
is pending. The [interrupted checkout page](/checkout/cancel) does not verify
payment status or cancel an existing payment.

If payment is confirmed but access is missing, use the recovery steps below.
Recovery does not complete an unpaid checkout. Support can review an uncertain
payment using your checkout email, product and approximate purchase date; do
not send card details or access links.

## Recover your download

1. Check the inbox and spam folder for the email address used at checkout.
2. Open [purchase recovery](/orders/recover), enter that same email address, and
   select **Resend access link**.
3. If eligible access is found and the request can be processed, PyColors sends
   a new claim email. Open its access link, check the product name, and download
   the ZIP from the claim page.
4. Unzip the package and continue with [Getting started](/docs/starter-pro/getting-started).

Access links currently expire 24 hours after they are issued. If a link expires
before you download, request a fresh one. Keep claim links private: they grant
access to your purchase.

<Callout title="What the confirmation means">
  The recovery page gives the same confirmation whether or not eligible access
  is found. It does not confirm that a purchase exists or that an email reached
  your inbox. Repeated requests may also be temporarily limited.
</Callout>

## If recovery does not work

### No email after the confirmation

Check the checkout email for typos and look in spam. If you made several requests,
wait at least 30 minutes before trying again. If it still does not arrive,
contact support.

### The form reports an error

Check your connection and try again later. If the error continues, contact
support; the error does not prove that your payment failed.

### The access link is unavailable or the download fails

Request a fresh link. If that also fails, contact support with the product name
and the error you see.

### The email points to another PyColors product

Recovery currently uses the most recent active purchase access associated with
the email. Contact support for help with your Starter Pro order.

### You cannot access the checkout email

Contact support. The recovery form cannot change the email address on a purchase.

Recovery cannot complete an unpaid checkout or restore access that is no longer
active. If you believe you paid but cannot access the product, contact support
before making another purchase.

## Ask for purchase support

Open [purchase support](/orders/support) for the email contact and instructions
if your email app does not open. Include:

- the email address used at checkout;
- the product name, **Starter Pro**;
- the approximate purchase date and order reference, if available;
- what happened and the steps you already tried.

Do not include passwords, payment card details, API keys, or claim/download links.
Remove those details from screenshots too. Support can review the access issue;
no response-time or resolution-time guarantee is promised.
